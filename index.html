LEGACY

Michael Jackson transcended music to become one of the most celebrated entertainers and humanitarians of the 20th century. His groundbreaking music videos, electrifying dance moves, and genre-defying sound redefined what popular music could be.

From the Jackson 5 to his legendary solo career, Jackson's artistry touched every corner of the globe. His album Thriller remains the best-selling album of all time, with over 66 million copies sold worldwide.
